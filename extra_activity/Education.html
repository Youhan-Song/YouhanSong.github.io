AVA: Autonomous Vehicle for All

The project is to bring the safety benefits of autonomous driving to all Americans by enabling Automated Driving Systems (ADS) in rural environments. The goal is to develop a systematic and scalable approach to the safe integration of automated vehicles into the nation's transportation system.

Among the various fields of autonomous systems, perception is one of the most significant. For perception, sensors including camera, lidar, and radar are used to detect the objects around the vehicle and transfer the information needed to take an action.

To develop a decent model, a large amount of real-world driving data was collected and annotated, and deep learning models were trained and evaluated to reliably perceive the surrounding environment.

Sponsor: Texas DOT (Department of Transportation)
